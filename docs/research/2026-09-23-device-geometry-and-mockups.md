# iPhone Duo device geometry, mockup assets, and shape language

Checked: September 23, 2026. Scope: display and physical geometry, poses, reserved regions, controls, Apple's design kits and bezels (contents and license), Liquid Glass shape rules as they apply on Duo, and third-party mockup resources. The goal is a concept prototype that matches the real device. This note builds on the [signal audit](2026-09-23-signal-audit.md) and the [source index](2026-09-23-source-index.md). It does not repeat the layout and control guidance summarized there.

Evidence tiers: **[A]** Apple official · **[V]** other official vendor · **[F]** firsthand report · **[R]** research study · **[D]** designer concept and opinion (this tier also covers press analysis by writers who did not handle the device). Confidence levels: **High** means Apple publishes the value. **Medium** means Apple data read out by a third party, or arithmetic on Apple numbers. **Low** means measured from Apple illustrations, or a community value.

## Short answer

- **Apple publishes only some of the geometry.** Apple publishes the panel pixels, pixel density, diagonals, dimensions, weight, and refresh rate. None of the documents I checked publish point sizes, corner radii, safe-area insets, the fold-region width, camera frames, or hinge-angle ranges ([specs](https://www.apple.com/iphone-duo/specs/), [HIG](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/designing-for-iphone-duo), [preparation guide](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/technologyoverviews/preparing-your-app-for-iphone-duo)) [A].
- **Apple's own beta tooling fills most of the gaps.** A developer read the device profile that ships with the Xcode 27.1 beta simulator. It gives the outer display as 466 × 678 pt and the inner display as 669 × 951 pt, both at @3x. The inner display is landscape-native. Outer corners are 8 pt on the hinge side and 59 pt on the free side; inner corners are 55 pt ([Crosley, Sept 21](https://blakecrosley.com/blog/xcode-27-1-beta-iphone-duo-simulator)) [F].
  - The inner canvas of 2007 × 2853 px matches Apple's App Store "device resolution" ([previews](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/app-preview-specifications), [screenshots](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/screenshot-specifications)) [A]. It is larger than the 1878 × 2670 panel, so the inner display renders at 3x and is then scaled down to the panel.
- **Physical size carries across displays.** By derivation, both displays show about 153 pt per inch, so type and controls keep their physical size when the device opens. Each half of the inner display (about 475 × 669 pt) is close to the size of the outer display (466 × 678 pt).
- **Apple does not publish angle thresholds for poses.** Apple names poses in three different vocabularies: the product page, the developer docs, and the hinge API. The API reports only `closed`, `partiallyOpen`, and `fullyOpen`, plus an angle in radians ([UIHinge](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/uikit/uihinge/angle)) [A]. "Tabletop" is not an Apple term; Apple's term is **Seated**.
- **Apple's bezels cover only flat views.** The Duo bezel pack is Photoshop and PNG only, with 2 finishes × 5 flat views. It has no book, seated, or standing views ([Crosley's inspection](https://blakecrosley.com/blog/iphone-duo-day-two)) [F].
  - Its license permits UI mock-ups for Apple-platform software. It forbids embedding the files in software and making derivative works. Apple's marketing rules also forbid 3D renders, tilting, and animation of the device ([DMG license](https://devimages-cdn.apple.com/design/resources/download/Bezel-iPhone-Duo.dmg), [marketing guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/marketing/guidelines/)) [A].
- **Shape language.** Liquid Glass uses three shape types: fixed, capsule, and concentric. A concentric shape's radius is the parent's radius minus the padding ([WWDC25 356](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2025/356/)) [A], and Apple's concentricity APIs are "updated to work with the screen shapes on iPhone Duo" ([Tech Talk 111461](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/tech-talks/111461/)) [A]. On Duo, the vertical bars are circular glass buttons and vertical capsules on a rail along the right edge.

## UI shape language

| Rule | What it means on Duo | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Three shape types | Fixed shapes have a constant radius. A capsule's radius is half its height. A concentric shape's radius is the parent's radius minus the padding. Capsules are used "throughout the system" in bars and buttons | [WWDC25 356](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2025/356/) [A] |
| Hardware sets the curvature | "The shape of the hardware informs the curvature of controls." ## Implications for the prototype

1. **Canvases.** Design at 466 × 678 pt for the outer display and 951 × 669 pt for the inner display, landscape-native (rotated: 669 × 951). Export at @3x: 1398 × 2034 and 2853 × 2007 px. These sizes fit Apple's bezel cutouts and App Store sizes exactly. Keep one type ramp in points for both displays, because a point is about the same physical size on each.
2. **Continuity model.** Each half of the inner display is about the size of the outer display. This supports the README's working hypothesis. The closed glance surface can reappear as one half of the book pose, and the hub's workspace or agent can take the other half.
3. **An asymmetric shell.** Reserve a right-edge rail for the Dynamic Island, the status cluster, and the bars on the outer display and in inner landscape. Use ≈ 80 pt as a placeholder until the kit or the simulator confirms a value. In inner portrait, switch to horizontal bars. With four hub areas plus search, the tab bar fits as one vertical capsule plus a search circle. Put the agent's primary action at the top of the toolbar only if it is navigation or a prominent action; otherwise keep it in its group.
4. **Corners.** Build every surface with concentric logic, not fixed radii. On the outer display, corners on the hinge side come out nearly square unless a minimum radius is set. Choose that minimum deliberately and record it as a prototype token.
5. **Fold.** Model the division region as a runtime value: 0 when open flat, and a band in the Book and Seated poses. Use 27 pt only as a labeled placeholder. Keep taps out of the band and use an even number of grid columns.
6. **Ambient station.** Apple's resting poses come in two forms. **Standing** uses the tent pose and the outer display in landscape, running StandBy. **Seated** uses the inner display in portrait: a distance-readable top region and a bottom region for controls. The README's single "tabletop" station should become these two variants. Build the ambient station in Figma or on the web, because StandBy does not run in the simulator.
7. **Hinge.** Drive layout from the hinge status and reserved regions. Use the angle only for effects. Any angle thresholds the concept needs will be our own assumptions and must be labeled as departures.
8. **Mockup assets.** Use Apple's bezels only for static images of flat views. Do not embed the bezel PNGs in a coded prototype, and do not tilt or animate them in public materials. Draw our own book, standing, and seated device outlines from this sheet and keep them internal. Avoid community files that repackage Apple's bezels or 3D model.
