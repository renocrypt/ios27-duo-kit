# Glass probe: frost against size (September 24, 2026)

Why large Liquid Glass frosts more than small, measured with the glass probe (`app/tools/glass-probe/`, `app/labs/probe.html`). The sizes scenes (`sizes-dark-x24`, `sizes-dark-x48`, layout `sizes` in `scenes.json`) put seven shapes over the same dark grating as the frost scenes (20 to 90, periods 24 and 48 pt, 8 phases). They were captured on the iPhone Duo simulator with iOS 27.1 on the outer display (466 × 678 pt, 3x), at the default slider position and at 0: after a reboot the simulated Duo is folded, and simctl cannot unfold it. The grating's 220 × 96 capsule and 360 × 180 panel, on the inner display at every slider position, complete the range. The material does not depend on the display (first probe note). The captures are in `/tmp/duo/glass-probe/captures/duo-27.1-outer/`.

"Contrast kept" is a shape's interior modulation (35% inset) over the 48 pt button's in the same capture.

## It follows the short side, not the area

Contrast kept at 24 pt:

| Shape | Short side | Slider 0 | Default |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 360 × 56 capsule | 56 | 1.00 | 1.04 |
| 56 × 150 capsule (grating) | 56 | 1.00 | 1.03 |
| 76 circle | 76 | 0.94 | 0.95 |
| 96 circle | 96 | 0.75 | 0.75 |
| 420 × 96 capsule | 96 | 0.74 | 0.74 |
| 220 × 96 capsule (grating) | 96 | 0.75 | 0.75 |
| 150 × 150, r 40 | 150 | 0.27 | 0.27 |
| 360 × 180, r 40 (grating) | 180 | 0.20 | 0.20 |

- 360 × 56 and 150 × 150 have the same area; only the square frosts more. The 96 circle and the 420 × 96 capsule differ in area by 4.4 times and frost alike. The corner radius is not it either: the 150 square (r 40) frosts far more than the 96 circle (r 48).
- The size's effect does not depend on the slider: at 24 pt the 220 × 96 capsule keeps 0.746, 0.746, 0.747, and 0.73 of the button's contrast at positions 0, 0.25, 0.5, and 0.75, and the panel 0.196, 0.203, 0.197, and 0.22. It acts as a filter on top of the slider's frost.
- It is not a wider Gaussian. At 48 pt the capsule keeps 0.87 (a Gaussian that keeps 0.75 at 24 pt keeps 0.93 at 48) and the 150 square 0.60 to 0.64 (0.72). A share of the content moved into a blur of about 9 to 10 pt fits both periods, with the same σ at every size.
- Clear glass does not frost more with size: a 360 × 180 clear panel keeps 0.998 to 1.001 of the 48 pt clear shape's contrast, at both periods and both slider positions.

## The model, fitted

Large regular glass replaces a share of its narrow frost with the content blurred by regular's σ and `sizeBlur` on top (`sizeShare` at `sizeStops`, tokens generated by `?calibrate`): σ 9.82 pt, shares 0, 0.07, 0.26, 0.77, and 0.85 at short sides 56, 76, 96, 150, and 180 pt, held beyond. Over 24 cases (six shapes, two periods, two slider positions), ours keeps the same contrast as iOS within 0.21 levels rms; the worst case is 0.43 levels (the 96 circle at 48 pt, default position).

Two things learned in the fitting:

- **The size blur comes before the lensing.** Blurred after it, as the narrow frost is, it raised a band of contrast 8 to 20 pt inside the rim where the lens magnifies the content: on the 150 square at the default position, 5.9 to 6.2 levels there against iOS's 2.8 to 3.4. Read at the source, as the wide blur is, it gives 2.7 to 3.2, and the interior is unchanged.
- **Weight the fit in levels.** A contrast of about 2 levels (the panels at the default position) was off by a few tenths, much more than the model predicts. The likely cause is 8-bit rounding: over an interior of uniform mean it does not average out. Relative error gave these cases as much weight as the ones at 5 to 20 levels.

## Open

- **The narrow frost at the rim.** Contrast against depth inside the rim, slider 0:

  | Depth (pt) | 1 | 3 | 5 | 12 | 45 |

  | 96 circle, iOS | 14.8 | 15.0 | 15.8 | 16.6 | 14.3 |
  | ours, narrow frost after the lensing (as now) | 7.8 | 8.4 | 12.1 | 16.0 | 14.7 |
  | ours, narrow frost before the lensing (trial) | 14.7 | 14.8 | 14.8 | 14.8 | 14.7 |

  The 48 pt button: iOS 20.3 at 1 pt, ours 15 (after) or 19.7 (before). Where the lens squeezes the content at the rim, a blur after it removes the contrast that iOS keeps. The first refit chose "after" for how the capsule ends look, and the per-tint frost errors fell with it. The contrast says "before". At full size the 96 circle looks the same either way (`inspect/2026-09-25/order-circle96-ios-pre-post.png`), so the choice needs a look at the ends at several slider positions, then a recalibration.
- **The lensing outlier was a measurement failure.** Where the narrow frost removes the rim's contrast, our phase cannot be read, so the calibration's centre-line lensing error on the 220 × 96 capsule (11.7 to 13 pt) is not the lensing. Along the 96 circle's centre line, iOS and ours agree within 1.7 pt at 1 pt deep and within 1 pt from 3 pt in.
- **Dark crescents.** iOS's large round glass shows dark crescents in the lensing band (`inspect/2026-09-25/calibrated-sizes-*.png`) that ours draws faintly, although displacement and contrast match. Possibly the band's darkening (the known vignette of 2 to 4 levels), larger on large shapes; not measured yet.
