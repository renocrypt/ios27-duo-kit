# Hub precedents: the AI agent and the ambient station

Checked: September 23, 2026. Scope: how agentic assistants and ambient/home displays are presented in 2025–2026 products and research, distilled into two evidence-backed pattern sets for the Duo hub concept: (A) an agent that acts across time, knowledge, and home while the person stays in control; (B) the tabletop pose as an "ambient station." Builds on the [signal audit](2026-09-23-signal-audit.md) and [source index](2026-09-23-source-index.md); Duo layout, reserved-region, and vertical-bar guidance is not repeated here.

**Evidence tiers:** **V** = official vendor; **F** = firsthand report or review; **F\*** = press report of an unannounced product (secondhand rumor, weakest); **R** = research study; **D** = designer opinion or essay. Sources marked *(search result)* could not be opened (HTTP 403, blocked, or JavaScript-only), so the claim rests on the search-result excerpt only.

## Short answer

- **Every 2026 agent that acts converges on the same control model:** it reads freely, then pauses before actions that are consequential, shared, or hard to undo, and it hands the person the controls for passwords and payments. Apple ([WWDC26-343](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2026/343/)), Google ([screen automation](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16940971?hl=en), [Spark](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16596215?hl=en&co=GENIE.Platform%3DAndroid)), and OpenAI ([ChatGPT Work](https://openai.com/index/chatgpt-for-your-most-ambitious-work/), search result) differ in wording, not in structure. The hub should adopt this model and decide *where on Duo* each step appears.
- **Asking for confirmation on every action defeats itself.** Brain responses to a repeated warning drop sharply after its second exposure ([Anderson et al., CHI 2015](https://scholarsarchive.byu.edu/facpub/9306/)). Apple scales confirmation to side effects and ownership: an event with attendees is confirmed, a personal one may not be ([WWDC26-343](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2026/343/)).
- **Participants in an agent study asked for a third choice besides approve and deny, namely to change the plan** ([Magentic-UI study](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.22358)). Plans that only *seem* plausible miscalibrate trust ([plan-then-execute, N = 248](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.01390)).
- **The failed AI gadgets failed on basics and stood apart from the phone.** The Humane AI Pin was slow, overheated, and could not handle timers or calendars; the Rabbit R1's interface ran as one Android app. On Duo, the agent should live inside the phone's own data and intents and should use the fold, not replace it.
- **Apple's pose vocabulary already splits the tabletop hypothesis in two.** *Standing* (tent) holds StandBy: clock, photos, and widgets, now without charging. *Seated* (propped open) holds active viewing, "with easy access to the controls on the bottom" ([product page](https://www.apple.com/iphone-duo/)). Apple's own talk assigns the top half to content "visible at a distance" and the bottom half to controls ([Tech Talk 111463](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/tech-talks/111463/)).
- **An ambient station should change its density with distance and attention.** Echo Show changes layout by proximity, Vogel and Balakrishnan's four interaction phases do the same, and a rumored Apple hub reportedly does too. The periphery should be governed by calm-technology principles: offer, don't demand; the person decides what moves to the center.

## 3. Pattern set A: the agent acts; the person stays in control

| # | Pattern | Evidence | What it means for Duo |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | **Consent scales with consequence, not with domain.** Reads and personal, reversible edits run; shared, external, costly, or irreversible actions pause; for a batch, use the highest risk. | WWDC26-343 ownership; ChatGPT "Important actions"; Magentic-UI irreversibility tags; habituation after the second exposure | One ladder for all four areas. *Time:* moving a personal block runs; changing an event with attendees asks. *Knowledge:* search and summarize run; share or export asks. *Home:* lights and media run; locks, garage, alarm, and cameras ask. |
| A2 | **Credentials and money stay with the person.** The agent prepares; the person completes. | Gemini pauses for passwords and payment; Pixel 11 hand-back at payment; OpenAI takeover *(search result)* | A "Your turn" card. On the outer display it requires Touch ID on the side button or opening the device, never a passive tap. Duo has Touch ID, not Face ID ([specs](https://www.apple.com/iphone-duo/specs/), V). |
| A3 | **Approvals offer three choices: Approve / Change / Decline**, and state the consequence, provenance, and whether it can be undone. | Magentic-UI "third option"; plan-then-execute trust miscalibration; Amershi guidelines 9 and 11 | The approval sheet is a core hub component. "Change" opens the plan on the inner display. |
| A4 | **Progress is visible and interruptible at a glance; detail is one step deeper.** | Gemini virtual window and Stop; Spark completed/current/planned steps; ChatGPT Work redirect | Closed: a Live Activity-style status with Stop on the outer display or Dynamic Island. Open: the full step list next to the affected content (Split View precedent). |
| A5 | **Forgiveness by default.** Reversible actions get an undo window; irreversible ones get a preview. | WWDC26-250; Gmail's 5–30 s send delay *([Google Help](https://support.google.com/mail/answer/2819488?hl=en&co=GENIE.Platform%3DDesktop), search result)* | A toast with Undo for home and time actions. A preview plus confirmation for messages and purchases. |
| A6 | **One ledger of what the agent did.** | Samsung "review AI actions in one place"; Siri app history synced across devices | A hub "Activity" view, filterable by area, where each entry links to its undo or follow-up. |
| A7 | **Autonomy is set per area and always shown.** | Feng et al. five levels | An autonomy setting per area (for example, Home = approver, Knowledge = consultant); show the current level on approval cards. |
| A8 | **Ground the agent in the phone's data; do the basics reliably.** | Humane and Rabbit failures; App Intents entities and onscreen awareness | The agent acts through intents and entities, including notifications and Now Playing. Timers, calendar, and lights must be instant before long tasks are shown. |
| A9 | **Generate UI from a fixed catalog, not free-form.** | A2UI pre-approved catalog; generative UI takes up to a minute and has errors; App Intents snippets | Agent answers are assembled from hub components (event, note, device, approval, progress), which keeps them fast, consistent, and testable. |
| A10 | **Proactivity goes to briefs and the periphery; interrupt only for a big mistake or urgency.** | Pixel Proactive Assistance cards; Now Brief; Home Brief; Alexa+ follow-ups; WWDC26-250 on interruptions | Morning and evening briefs on the outer display or StandBy; agent suggestions appear as passive cards and are never pushed. |

## 4. Pattern set B: the tabletop pose as an ambient station

| # | Pattern | Evidence | What it means for Duo |
|---|---|---|---|
| B1 | **Split by posture: Standing is ambient, Seated is the station.** | Product page pose names; MacRumors tent StandBy | Refine the README hypothesis. *Standing (tent):* calm faces on the outer display that extend StandBy. *Seated (propped inner):* an interactive station. Which display serves which pose is our inference; Apple says StandBy uses whichever display faces up. |
| B2 | **Top half for reading at a distance; bottom half for touch; nothing interactive at the crease.** | Tech Talk 111463; Samsung Flex guidance; MacRumors on the crease | Seated layout: top shows now and next, home status, and agent status; bottom shows media transport, scene buttons, and approvals. |
| B3 | **Density follows distance and attention.** | Echo Show proximity layouts; Vogel's four phases; rumored Apple hub scaling; StandBy widget enlargement | Two levels at minimum: *far* (one or two large facts) and *near* (after a tap or approach: details and controls). The ambient layer stays as the anchor and is never replaced. |
| B4 | **Calm by default: offer, don't demand; the person decides what comes to the center.** | Weiser & Brown; Case; Mankoff "peripherality" | Slow updates, no motion loops, a Night Mode red tint, and abstract state such as a glyph showing whether the agent is busy. Stays useful offline, as a clock and last-known state. |
| B5 | **Each face answers one question within a glance.** | ~5-second glances (Gouveia); bar and donut charts beat radial bars (Blascheck) | Use numerals, bars, and donuts; avoid radial bars and dense lists. Each face has one job (time, next event, home, agent). |
| B6 | **Controls favor media and simple toggles; secure devices require the owner.** | Ammari (music, search, IoT; 85% on/off); Pixel Tablet limits while locked; Echo Home/Away/Night modes | The bottom half offers media, 3–4 favorite toggles, and a scene mode. Locks and cameras require authentication (A1 and A2). |
| B7 | **Shared-room privacy: only harmless personal information until the owner is present.** | Vogel "harmless" information; Pixel Tablet guest voice; Echo Visual ID | Show free/busy status, not titles or message bodies. Agent approvals are hidden while the device is locked. |
| B8 | **The station hosts routine check-ins.** | IMWUT 2026 family display; Home Brief | Daily brief, household calendar, and agent digest are delivered here, not as notifications. |

## 5. Implications for the hub concept

1. **Adopt one consent ladder (A1–A3) as a design-system primitive.** It is the strongest convergent signal across Apple, Google, OpenAI, and research. The distinctive Duo contribution is posture-aware approval: glance-level status on the outer display, a "Change" path on the inner display, and authentication for anything involving money, credentials, or security.
2. **Revise the pose hypothesis in the README when the design lead agrees.** Use "standing = ambient (outer)" and "seated = station (inner split)" rather than one "tabletop" state. This matches Apple's own vocabulary and talk.
3. **Build the ambient layer on StandBy, not beside it.** Duo StandBy already runs without charging on either display. The hub's faces for time, home, and agent should look like StandBy faces: backgrounds removed, enlarged, Night Mode.
4. **Put the agent beside content, not in place of it.** The fold enables Siri-style Split View; A8 argues against a separate "agent app" that owns the whole screen.
5. **Departures to mark as concept-only.** Hub-owned full StandBy faces (the reviewed sources show third-party widgets and Live Activities in StandBy, but not third-party faces); agent tasks lasting longer than the 8-hour Live Activity limit; proximity sensing in StandBy (the sensor is undocumented); agent control of locked or secure home devices; cross-app action beyond App Intents, as with Gemini-style screen automation.

## 6. Open questions and limits

- **Duo hardware unknowns.** Hinge-angle ranges, position sensors, whether StandBy can detect presence, and the battery cost of running StandBy without charging are all undocumented ([Mac Observer](https://www.macobserver.com/news/iphone-duo-posable-positions-rebuilt-standby-what-apple-documents/)). The simulator lacks StandBy (see the [signal audit](2026-09-23-signal-audit.md)).
- **Siri AI is beta,** waitlisted, rate-limited, English-first, and initially unavailable on iOS in the EU and in China; agent patterns cannot yet be observed at scale on Duo.
- **Apple home hub and homeOS are rumors (F\*).** Whether a Duo station should hand off to such a hub, or overlap with it, cannot be settled until Apple announces it.
- **Evidence gaps.** No long-term field study of agent approvals was found; the habituation evidence comes from security warnings, not agents. The Magentic-UI study (12 people, 1 hour) and plan-then-execute (a simulated environment) are short-term. Glance studies are on smartwatches, not tabletop phones.
- **Unopened pages.** OpenAI pages returned HTTP 403, and The Verge was not fetchable; those claims rely on search excerpts and are marked. Apple HIG pages (Duo, Widgets, Generative AI) render only through JavaScript and could not be re-read here.
- **Not verified.** Pixel 11 claims that the interface shows "where it found the information" appeared only on low-tier sites and were excluded. Samsung's "Now Bar" was not found in its official July 2026 material.
